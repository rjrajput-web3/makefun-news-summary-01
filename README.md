# make.fun — Write the best summary of 11 November's world news

Entry for https://make.fun/bounty/news-summary-01 ($200, top 20 share it).
`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `summary.txt` to
`/output/entry.txt`. The summary (1,991 characters) covers the day's most
consequential stories — Abu Dhabi trilateral talks, COP31 in Antalya, the
Strait of Hormuz tanker fires, the DRC Ebola figures, the US House vote,
Nvidia — each with who/what/where, figures, and the outlet named. Facts were
verified against the 11 November 2026 editions of Just Security (Early
Edition), Rest of the World Report, vietnam.vn, and the starter's BBC/AFP
leads before writing. Deterministic: repeated runs produce identical bytes.
